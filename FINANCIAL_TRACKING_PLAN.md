
# HAVEN PROPERTY SOLUTIONS
## Financial Tracking System

Currency: KES (Kenyan Shillings)

### 1. Revenue Sources
- Commission from property sales
- Commission from property rentals
- Profit from buying and reselling properties
- Other business income

### 2. Completed Deals
Each deal will record:
- Property name
- Transaction type: Sale, Rental, or Resale
- Transaction date
- Selling or rental amount
- Property purchase cost (if applicable)
- Commission earned
- Direct transaction expenses
- Payment received
- Payment status

### 3. Business Expenses
Record:
- Marketing and advertising
- Transport
- Property repairs and maintenance
- Office and administration
- Other business expenses

### 4. Financial Calculations
Commission Income = Commission Earned

Property Trading Profit =
Selling Price - Purchase Cost - Direct Expenses

Net Business Profit =
Total Revenue - Business Expenses

Commission and trading profit must not be
double-counted for the same transaction.

### 5. Financial Dashboard
Display:
- Total Revenue (KES)
- Total Expenses (KES)
- Net Profit (KES)
- Outstanding Payments (KES)
- Monthly Financial Performance

### 6. Security
- Financial records accessible only to authenticated admins
- Store records in MongoDB Atlas
- Validate financial amounts on the backend
- Preserve existing property and enquiry systems
